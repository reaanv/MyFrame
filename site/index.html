MyFrame

Turn a Xiaomi Android TV stick into an art and photo frame.

Download MyFrame 1.1.0 (APK) Source on GitHub

Android TV / Google TV, Android 8 or newer. About 15 MB. Free, no ads, no account.

What it does

Art Mode

22 public-domain paintings from The Met, shown in frames and mounts. More paintings arrive over Wi-Fi.

My Photos

Your own pictures: albums, favourites, a hand-picked set or a slideshow.

Mixed

Paintings and photos together, saved as presets with their own frame and timing.

Phone upload

Send photos from a phone over your home Wi-Fi. No app and no cloud account.

Music shortcuts

Tiles open the YouTube, Spotify and YouTube Music apps already installed on the stick.

Built for a small stick

Memory and storage are capped for 2 GB RAM and 8 GB storage. See Limits.

Setup

  1. On the stick: finish Google TV setup and join Wi-Fi.
  2. Enable debugging: open Settings, About, and press the build number seven times. Then turn on debugging in Developer options.
  3. Install from a computer with adb: 
    adb connect <stick-ip>:5555
adb install -r MyFrame.apk

    Some sticks use Wireless debugging instead. Then use adb pair and adb connect with the addresses shown on the TV.

  4. Open MyFrame from the TV's app row.
  5. Optional, make MyFrame the Home screen: run this from the GitHub repository. 
    bash tools/appliance.sh apply

    bash tools/appliance.sh undo puts the stock launcher back.

Full setup guide

Phone upload

On the TV open Photos, Add photos. Scan the QR code with a phone on the same Wi-Fi and type the 6-digit PIN. The page opens at http://<stick-ip>:8080.

The upload page runs on your stick, inside your home network. This website cannot reach your stick, and it never sees your photos. Open the address shown on your TV, from a phone on the same Wi-Fi.

Guest Wi-Fi or client isolation can block it. Do not forward port 8080 on your router.
